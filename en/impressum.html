4. Data collection on this website

Cookies

This website does not use cookies and does not employ any analytics, tracking or marketing tools. No information is stored on your device that would require consent under § 25 TDDDG. For this reason, you are not shown a cookie banner either.

The only thing stored is your explicit choice of language, if you make one — a single entry in your browser's local storage containing nothing but the language code. It is strictly necessary for the presentation you asked for and therefore exempt from consent; see the section "Language detection and language choice" for details.

External fonts

This website does not embed fonts from external servers. All fonts used are delivered locally by our hosting provider. No connection to third-party servers — in particular to Google Fonts — takes place.

Audience measurement (own, anonymised statistics)

We use a self-operated, anonymised visitor statistic in order to evaluate the use of our website (e.g. page views, illustrations clicked, approximate region of origin). No cookies are set and no information is stored on your device in the process; the measurement is therefore not subject to consent under § 25 TDDDG.

The following is recorded, and nothing else: the type of event (e.g. page view or image click), the image or category concerned, the viewing duration, browser language, referring website (referrer), device class (mobile/desktop) as well as the country and city derived from the request. Language and device class are taken from the headers that your browser sends with every page request anyway; for the audience measurement, no information is read from your device. Independently of this, the website's language switching reads your browser's language setting — see the section "Language detection and language choice".

Your IP address is not stored. To distinguish visitors, only a short value (hash) is formed, into which — alongside the IP address — a secret key and the respective date are incorporated. This value changes daily, so that recognition across several days is ruled out. No profiling, no merging with other data sources and no cross-site tracking takes place.

The processing is carried out on the basis of our legitimate interest in the statistical evaluation and improvement of our online offering (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The statistics run on servers of Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. A data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR exists with Cloudflare; the transfer to the USA takes place on the basis of the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Language detection and language choice

This website is available in five languages (German, English, Spanish, French, Italian). So that you arrive at the version that suits you without a detour, a small script reads your browser's language setting (navigator.languages) when the page is loaded and redirects you once to the corresponding language version.

Only this language setting is evaluated. Your location, your IP address and your device's time zone are not taken into account — a device set to German receives the German version, even when it is abroad. The value determined is not transmitted to us and is not stored.

If you explicitly choose a version via "Language" at the bottom of the page, your browser remembers that decision in an entry in its local storage (localStorage, key pp_lang). This entry contains nothing but the language code, such as de or fr — no identifier, no date, nothing by which a visitor could be recognised. Without it, the automatic detection would override your choice again on the next page load.

Legal basis: reading the language setting and storing your explicit language choice are strictly necessary for the presentation of the website you asked for, and are therefore exempt from consent under § 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG; no cookie banner is required for this. Any further processing is based on our legitimate interest in an intelligible presentation of our offering (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

You can delete the entry at any time via your browser settings ("clear site data"). The automatic detection then applies again.
